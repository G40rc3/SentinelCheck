Choose how to send

    
BCC means blind carbon copy. Recipients do not see one another's addresses. Use BCC, never CC, for a bulk message. A single email might exceed your provider's recipient limit and does not prove delivery or completion. Individual requests are easier to track.

    

      
COPY ADDRESSES
One BCC draft
Copy listed email addresses, paste them into BCC in your own email app, then paste the request text. Check recipients and limits before you press Send. Your email app may ask you to put your own address in To.

      
TEXT FILE
Keep an editable list
Download the addresses and individual draft messages. Open the file, verify contacts, then use it for BCC, mail merge or separate emails.

      
EMAIL APP
Individual drafts
Open pre-filled emails in your default app. Open five drafts per click. Review and send each one yourself; browsers and email apps may block a large batch.
